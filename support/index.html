How we can help

01

Report a problem

Describe what happened and roughly when. Include a screenshot if you can — never personal details from a call.

02

Get unstuck

Camera permission, dark video, matching trouble or a broken friend code — someone in the group can help.

03

Follow releases

The channel is the fastest way to know when a fix or a new feature goes live.

Stay anonymous. Kismet has no accounts by design. Never post your real name, phone number, address or account details in a public Telegram group — moderators will never ask you for them. For safety reports, include only the date, approximate time and what happened.
